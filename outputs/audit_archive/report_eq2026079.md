On November 9, 2026, at 12:21 local time, an M4.2 earthquake occurred in the Alishan region (23.59°N, 120.72°E) at a depth of 11.0 km. In a post-hoc arrival-time replay, EdgeQuake produced its first location at origin+4.5s and its first magnitude estimate of 2.98 at origin+6.3s. As additional arrivals arrived, the magnitude evolved to 4.22, with a final epicenter position error of 6.5 km relative to the official CWA report.

The EEW criteria were not met. The PWS public alert did not trigger: the magnitude/intensity rule ((M>=5.0 & county intensity>=4) or (M>=6.5 & >=3)) was never met in any single instant — the magnitude estimate peaked at M4.24 and the highest predicted county intensity was 2 (these extremes need not be simultaneous). Based on WorldPop 2026 R2025A data and point-source average-site assumptions, an estimated ~167872 people may have felt intensity-3+ shaking. A comparable past event was the 2017-11-22T14:20 M5.1 earthquake 23 km S of Lugu, Taiwan. This report presents lower-bound estimate results from an automated research prototype and is not an official warning.
